    Why Visual Identification ≠ Local Recycling Acceptance

    Many apps guess a bin color just because an item looks like plastic or paper. In reality, municipal recovery facilities only accept specific resin codes, unsoiled materials, and segregated components. WasteWise separates AI Material Recognition from Verified Local Guide Lookup.

    🌱

    Biodegradable / Wet Waste

    Hex Gradient: #1F8A4C → #69B578

    biodegradable

    When used: Food scraps, raw fruit/vegetable peels, tea leaves, and compostable organic matter explicitly accepted by your local guide.

    Watch out for: "Bio-plastic" (PLA) cups or compostable bags are often rejected by standard municipal composting unless industrial composting is explicitly supported in your local guide.

    ♻️

    Dry Waste / Potentially Recyclable

    Hex Gradient: #1769AA → #5BA7E8

    dry_recyclable

    When used: Rinsed metal beverage cans, clean corrugated cardboard, PET (#1) and HDPE (#2) bottles, and separated dry mono-layer wrappers where supported.

    Watch out for: Food-soiled pizza boxes, greasy foil sachets, and multi-layer laminated pouches contaminate dry recycling loads.

    🛡️

    Hazardous / Special Handling Alert

    Hex Gradient: #B4232F → #F26B73

    special_handling

    Critical Distinction: This red banner is WasteWise's visual alert theme — not an instruction to put all special items into a single physical red bin.

    Subtype Routes: Batteries & e-waste go to authorized E-Waste Kiosks; needles, lancets, and expired medicines go to Biomedical/Health Center take-back points; chemical/paint containers go to hazardous waste drives.

    ❓

    Unresolved / Needs Clarification

    Hex Gradient: #667085 → #D0D5DD

    unresolved

    When used: Blurry/dark photos, multi-part packaging waiting for user clarification, items with no matching rule in the active local guide, or conflicting guide passages.

    Safety promise: WasteWise never forces an uncertain item into green, blue, or red just to look complete.
